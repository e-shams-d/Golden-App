## Resolution protocol

- `Resolved — Approved` means an implementation choice has explicit recorded approval and
  a normative M0 baseline. Original conflicting source passages remain evidence of why the
  decision was required and must be editorially synchronized in their next revision.
- Implementation completion is separate from decision resolution: migrations, schema/API
  contracts, tests, and task references must still demonstrate the approved baseline at the
  affected milestone gate.
- A safe interim rule is containment only. It must not be treated as the final product or architecture decision.
- Financial, security, privacy, retention, and production conflicts block the affected implementation point.
- Unrelated reversible foundation work may continue when it does not embed the disputed choice.
